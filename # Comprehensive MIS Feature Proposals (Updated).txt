# Comprehensive MIS Feature Proposals (Updated)

Here is the updated list of potential features (modules) for your universal platform, incorporating your latest additions. Every major numbered feature here represents a dedicated ASP.NET Core Area.

## Core Administration & People
1. **User Management & Performance (KPIs)**
   A robust directory for all staff/members. Admins can manually add custom Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to evaluate users. It tracks attendance and ranks profiles to help identify the "best staff".
2. **Committees & Sub-Groups**
   Private, isolated spaces for specific teams (like a Finance Committee) to share files and discuss.
3. **Alumni Networking Directory**
   A searchable portal for past members to connect, offer mentorship, and post business opportunities.

## Reports & Documentation
4. **Dynamic Interval Reporting**
   Admins can create report categories (e.g., "Daily Staff Report") and set strict time intervals. The system automatically flags "lateness" or missed submissions.
5. **Interactive User Manual**
   A dedicated help module built into the system. It breaks down every feature and explains exactly what each user role is supposed to do and what specific buttons do.
6. **Custom Form Builder & Surveys**
   A dynamic tool exactly like Google Forms. Admins can drag and drop different types of questions (multiple choice, text, checkboxes) to create questionnaires, data collection forms, or internal surveys.
7. **Survey Analytics & Graphical Reporting**
   The companion to the Form Builder. When users fill out the custom forms, the Admin dashboard automatically generates pie charts, bar graphs, and graphical representations of the answers.

## Financials & Monetization
8. **Event & Concert Ticketing**
   A comprehensive ticketing engine for concerts, seminars, or community gatherings. It handles paid and free ticket generation, RSVP limits, and barcode/QR code scanning.
9. **Financials & Dues Management**
   Handles the collection of mandatory subscriptions and recurring membership dues. It tracks defaulters and automates payment reminders.
10. **Proposal Pipeline (Internal Ideas & Pitches)**
    A workflow engine for staff to pitch project ideas, upload budgets, and get management approval.
11. **Grant Ledger & Funding Tracking**
    Crucial for NGOs and Agencies to track external awarded funding, disbursement milestones, and donor reporting deadlines.

## Operations & Workflow
12. **Recruitment & Applicant Tracking (ATS)**
    Manages the full hiring lifecycle from job postings to virtual/physical interview scheduling and candidate scoring.
13. **Elections & E-Voting**
    Highly secure digital balloting system guaranteeing one-user-one-vote with real-time result tabulation.
14. **Fleet & Equipment Lifecycle Management**
    An advanced tracking system specifically for tech gadgets, heavy equipment, or vehicles. It logs exactly when an item was bought, tracks duration of use, logs maintenance history, and calculates depreciation.
15. **Company Asset Management**
    A broader ledger for tracking high-level assets owned by the company (like real estate, intellectual property, or fixed corporate assets) ensuring everything is properly accounted for and valued on the balance sheet.
16. **Project & Task Tracking**
    Breaks initiatives into assignable tasks and provides leadership with visual completion dashboards.

## Communication & Tech Integrations
17. **Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) Artificial Intelligence**
    A powerful module allowing clients to input their own API keys for Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT. It unlocks AI capabilities (e.g., AI drafting newsletters) at no extra AI cost to you.
20. **Mass Communication (Newsletter/SMS)**
    Built-in broadcast engine for targeted emails or urgent text alerts, complete with open-rate tracking.
21. **Anonymous Whistleblowing & Feedback**
    An untraceable portal for staff/members to submit sensitive complaints or suggestions securely.
22. **Helpdesk & Ticketing System**
    A structured way for users to request support or report bugs, tracking staff resolution times.

## Compliance & Development
23. **Document Management System (DMS)**
    A secure vault for official policies and board minutes, featuring strict access logs and version control.
24. **Learning Management System (LMS)**
    Hosts internal training videos and compliance tests, issuing digital certificates upon completion.
25. **Visitor & Gate Management**
    A digital logbook for security desks to register guests, capture photos, and notify hosts of arrivals.
